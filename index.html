Three levels. Same obsessive detail.

Level 0 · The Lobby

Mono-yellow wallpaper, water-stained drop ceilings, dying troffers and outlets that still spark. Follow scrawled arrows — most of them tell the truth.

Level 1 · Habitable Zone

A foggy concrete expanse of pillars, puddles and supply crates. Periodic blackouts roll across the floor toward you.

Level 2 · Pipe Dreams

Hot, narrow maintenance tunnels choked with pipes, steam and red emergency lights. Something knocks back.

Things that share the space

Crawlers

Pale, too-long limbs, no eyes. They hunt by sound. Walk. Crouch. Don't sprint past their room.

The Watcher

It only moves when no one is looking. Keep it in view and back away slowly.

Smilers

A grin in the dark. They hate being lit. Point your flashlight at one, and run.

Mimics

Looks like one of your friends. Wears their name. Walks a little wrong.

Pipe-dwellers

You'll hear them knocking along the pipes long before you see them.

Get lost together

  • Public worlds with region-based matchmaking (NA, SA, EU, Africa, Asia, Oceania). Worlds fill up to 8 players, then overflow into the next one.
  • Custom rooms: pick a name (and optional password) and send friends your own invite link.
  • Proximity voice in 3D: muffled through walls, echoing down corridors. Push-to-talk or open mic.
  • Text chat with a bad-word filter you can toggle.
  • AI mode: two capable companions explore with you offline. No chat, board or voice, but you still earn coins.

Multiplayer is peer-to-peer (WebRTC). There's no game server to pay for — that's how it stays free. A few very strict networks may block direct connections.

The Base

Everyone spawns in a lit, quiet safe zone. Entities can't come in.

  • Supply Kiosk: Almond Water, batteries, a heavy-duty torch, outfits.
  • Lockers: stash items so you don't lose them when you're taken.
  • Bulletin Board: pin a note for everyone in your world. Costs 20 Backrooms Coins, and notes are filtered to keep them friendly.

Backrooms Coins (BC) are earned only by playing: exploring, surviving, finding Almond Water and escaping. There is no store and no real money, ever.

Made the hard way

Modelled in Blender

Every outlet, troffer, vent, door, locker and creature is built and rigged by scripts running Blender, then exported to glTF.

Procedural materials

Wallpaper, carpet, ceiling tiles, concrete and rust are generated as tileable PBR maps: colour, normal, roughness and AO.

Baked light fields

Hundreds of fluorescent fixtures with soft wall occlusion, flicker circuits and power-down waves, cheap enough for laptops.

Synthesised sound

Footsteps on damp carpet, 120 Hz ballast hum, breathing and heartbeat, all designed from DSP with HRTF 3D audio and room reverb.

Camcorder feel

Real motion blur, gait-driven head motion, handheld tremor, and a subtle VHS pass: grain, chroma bleed, tracking.

Runs almost anywhere

Auto quality presets, dynamic resolution and texture tiers. Tested on integrated GPUs and Apple Silicon.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no accounts, no purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.

What do I need?

A keyboard and mouse (or a gamepad) and a modern browser with WebGL2. Headphones make it much scarier.

How do I play with friends?

Open Online → Create Room, choose a name, then press TAB in game to copy the invite link. Or join a public world in your region.

Controls?

WASD move · Shift sprint · C crouch · Space jump · F flashlight · E interact · Q almond water · R battery · T chat · V push-to-talk · Tab players/pause.

Where did the Backrooms come from?

The Backrooms began as an anonymous 2019 creepypasta built around a single photo, and grew through a huge community of writers and artists. This game is a fan work inspired by that shared mythology.
