# Open Backrooms: design prompt

This is the brief the game is built from. You can reuse it as a prompt to recreate or extend the project.

> Build a browser game that recreates the Backrooms with insane realism, while still running on typical PCs and MacBooks.
>
> **World.** Level 0 is an endless maze of mono-yellow wallpapered rooms under a water-stained drop ceiling of 2×2 ft acoustic tiles and 2×4 fluorescent troffers. The carpet is damp and mustard-coloured. Some troffers flicker, some are dead, some hang out of the grid. There are wall outlets everywhere: duplex, old two-prong, GFCI, and broken ones hanging by their wires that spark. Scrawled arrows point (mostly) toward the exit. Level 1 is a foggy concrete parking-garage and warehouse with pillars, puddles, crates and rolling blackouts. Level 2 is hot, narrow maintenance tunnels choked with painted, rusting pipes, steam and red emergency lights. Each level is infinite and deterministic from a seed.
>
> **Base.** Players spawn in a lit safe zone with a supply kiosk, lockers and a bulletin board. Entities can't enter. Players earn in-game Backrooms Coins (never real money) and can pay coins to pin filtered notes on the board.
>
> **Entities.** Crawlers hunt by sound. The Watcher moves only when unobserved. Smilers lurk in the dark and charge when lit. Mimics wear a real player's name. Pipe-dwellers knock through the pipes. A tension director paces encounters.
>
> **Feel.** Human walking speeds with acceleration. Gait-synced head bob, sway and roll. Breathing, handheld tremor, and landing and bump springs. Stamina and crouch. Camera motion blur. A subtle camcorder/VHS pass with grain, chroma bleed and a rare tracking line: authentic, not old.
>
> **Sound.** Positional 120 Hz ballast hum, footsteps per surface, breathing and heartbeat, sparks, distant knocks and howls, HRTF 3D audio with wall occlusion and room reverb.
>
> **Multiplayer.** Free and serverless: public worlds with region matchmaking, custom rooms with invite links, proximity voice, and chat with a profanity filter. AI mode offers offline companions with no chat, board or voice, but still earns coins.
>
> **Assets.** Model everything in Blender via scripts. Generate textures and sounds procedurally.
>
